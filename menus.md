# OBU campus menus (reference for receipt cleanup)

Use these exact item names. Sizes, flavors, milk swaps and add-ons go in "detail", not the name.
"Meal"/"Combo" means the item plus a side and drink: name the main item and put "Meal" in detail.
A fountain drink at any spot ("SODA", "Soft Drink", "24 OZ FOUNTAIN") is "Fountain Drink".
